Professional MEP Engineering Training

One course that covers the work our consultancy does every day: HVAC, firefighting and plumbing design, drafted in AutoCAD and modelled in Revit MEP. Taught by the engineers who design those systems on live projects across the GCC.

Not academic. Taught from live projects.

  • Working engineers teach. Instructors come from our consultancy, where they design these systems for clients.
  • Real drawing sets. You work on project-style sets drawn from the building types we design in the GCC.
  • Reviewed like our own team. Your drawings go through the same design review our engineers face.

Five modules, one drawing set.

Each module ends with drawings and calculations you hand in for review. By the last one you have a complete MEP set for one building.

  1. M

    HVAC

    • Heat load calculations for GCC climates
    • Equipment selection: chilled water, VRF/VRV, DX
    • Duct sizing and layout
    • Ventilation, exhaust and smoke management
    • Pressurisation systems
  2. FP

    Firefighting

    • Hazard classification and system selection
    • Sprinkler, hose reel and hydrant design
    • Fire pump room and water tank sizing
    • Hydraulic calculations
    • Clean agent and kitchen hood suppression
  3. P

    Plumbing

    • Water supply and fixture unit calculations
    • Hot water circulation
    • Drainage, sewerage and storm design
    • Tank and booster pump sizing
    • Riser and schematic diagrams
  4. CAD

    AutoCAD

    • MEP drafting standards and layers
    • Layouts, sections and details
    • Shop drawings and builders’ work
    • Sheet setup and issue
    • PDF-to-CAD conversion
  5. BIM

    Revit MEP

    • Templates, families and worksharing
    • Modelling ducts, pipes and equipment
    • Clash detection and coordination
    • Sheets and schedules from the model
    • BIM-based quantity takeoff

What changes when working engineers teach.

An academic course teaches the theory. We teach the job: codes as authorities apply them, the details contractors ask about, and the comments that come back from Civil Defence.

Questions engineers ask us.

Do I need work experience?

No. The course suits final-year students and fresh graduates in mechanical, civil or electrical engineering, and working engineers who want to move into MEP design. A short skills check puts you at the right level.

Is it one course or several?

One course. HVAC, firefighting, plumbing, AutoCAD and Revit MEP are taught together, the way they come together on a real drawing set.

Who teaches it?

Engineers from Mechbridge International MEP Consultants who design these systems on current projects.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes. Each certificate carries a unique code. Employers can check it on our verification page.

Can I train while I work?

Yes. Sessions are scheduled around a working week, so most engineers train alongside their jobs.

What does it cost and when does the next batch start?

Published batches are listed on this page with their start dates. Send an enquiry and we will reply with the fee and the current schedule.
