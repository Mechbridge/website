# Generated website images

68 illustrative photos were generated with the built-in image generation tool using each subject, ratio and minimum size in IMAGES.md. Saved to public/images/ with the exact requested filenames. The supplied hero-home.webp was retained. og-default.jpg is a 1200 × 630 crop of that hero with the existing cream SVG logo at bottom left.

These are fictional illustrative people, buildings and operations, not verified photographs of actual staff, historical events, projects or office entrances.

## Prompt set

Each generation used the corresponding IMAGES.md subject, dimensions and ratio, followed by: “Low key moody golden hour, blue hour or warm practical interior lights; deep navy charcoal shadows, warm cream highlights, one naturally occurring red accent. No filter or color cast. Candid working people with diverse UAE, Saudi and Indian backgrounds. Every construction site person wears hard hat, high visibility vest and safety boots. Main subject central 60% for mobile cropping. No visible logos, client names, project names or watermarks. Illustrative fictional people and locations, not documentary evidence. Portraits: consistent warm office lighting and navy background. No collage; single full frame photograph.”
