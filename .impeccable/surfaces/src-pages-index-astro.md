# Surface: marketing site (home + training + services + about + contact)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: engineers choosing GCC-ready training (primary), developers/contractors needing MEP consultancy. Action: training enquiry (form or connect@). Proof: codes, authorities, disciplines, Dubai/KSA project exposure, 15+ years. Constraints: no invented figures; glass only over photo/clay.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the project office, not a brochure. Every page reads like a sheet set coming off a live Gulf project, with training sitting inside that office as its headline product. It refuses the category default of a stock-photo hero, a three-card services row and a "why choose us" grid.

OWN-WORLD: Midnight-navy ground with whole cream and vintage-red fields that own regions. Bricolage Grotesque in heavy condensed widths for headlines. Geist for body text, Geist Mono for code references and drawing callouts, Doto dot-matrix for indices and live badges. Components look like drawing-sheet title blocks: hairline rules, sheet refs (M-101, FP-201), revision tables. Frosted glass and fluted Nothing-style glass appear only over the hero photo and the clay scene.

STORY: The visitor sees a serious regional engineering group (Dubai, India, KSA). Then they learn its engineers teach, and that this is the only training run from inside live projects. They pick a track and enquire.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The night-house photo is full bleed. A cream logo sits top left in the frosted nav. Bottom left, a massive two-line headline at about 9vw, a short sub, a red "Explore training" button and a cream ghost "Consultancy" link. Bottom right, a frosted title-block panel lists the Dubai, India and KSA offices plus "Training enrolling". On mobile the house stays in frame and the text stacks below its roofline.

FORM: Brief-pinned world (brand theme + supplied hero + clay scroll scene + glass). Signature interaction: after the hero, the clay story pieces build and squash with scroll behind ten sections whose ground colours shift. Motion grammar: GSAP ScrollSmoother on desktop, one orchestrated hero reveal, then scrubbed line-mask reveals of headings. seed: none (pinned): "use the attached hero image on landing and after landing viewport the scrolling background starts", "add modern glassmorphism and colouminsed glass abstarct(Nothing os wallpaper like design as well)".

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
