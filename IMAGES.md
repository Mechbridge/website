# Image brief: mechbridgegroup.com

46 image slots across the public site (the admin portal has none). Every slot already has a file except those marked **needed**.

To replace one: export a WebP at the minimum size or larger, name it exactly as the File column says, put it in `public/images/` and rebuild. Keep files under about 350 KB.

## Rules for every photo

- **No team names, portraits or team photos.** People may appear in work scenes but never as identified Mechbridge staff, and alt text must not say they are.
- The look follows the hero: night or blue hour, deep navy shadows, warm interior light, and one natural red accent (fire pipework, valve wheels, red edge lighting).
- Real drawings, models, plant rooms and buildings. No client names or logos in frame.
- Mobile crops use the middle 60% of the frame.
