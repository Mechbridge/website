# Image brief: mechbridgegroup.com

The site has **68 image slots** plus one social sharing image. Right now each slot shows a blueprint-grid placeholder with its file name printed on it.

## How to add an image

1. Export the photo as **WebP** (JPG or PNG also work), at the minimum size listed or larger.
2. Name it exactly as the **File** column says (for example `track-hvac.webp`).
3. Put it in `public/images/`.
4. Run `npm run build` (or reload the dev server). The placeholder is replaced automatically. No code changes needed.

Keep each file under about 350 KB. Most photos land at 150 to 300 KB as WebP at quality 75 to 80.

## Art direction for every photo

The attached hero sets the look: a night-time building, low key, warm interior light, and a **thin edge of Mechbridge red**. Match it.

- **Light:** low key and moody. Golden hour, blue hour or warm interior practicals. Avoid flat, bright, overcast stock.
- **Colour:** deep navy and charcoal shadows, warm creams in highlights, and one red accent wherever it occurs naturally (fire pipework, a red pen, a hard hat, a red jacket, a valve wheel). Don't add a colour cast or a filter.
- **People:** real Mechbridge engineers and trainees wherever possible. Mixed backgrounds that reflect the UAE, KSA and India. People working, not posing at the camera. Hard hats, high-vis vests and safety boots on every site shot.
- **Subjects:** real drawings, models and plant rooms. Blur, redact or angle any client name, project name or logo out of frame.
- **Avoid:** handshake stock, generic skyline stock with no people, staged "thumbs up" shots, and visible watermarks.
- **Mobile crops:** most slots are cropped by `object-fit: cover`. Keep the main subject in the middle 60% of the frame.

## Shortcut if you're short on photos

Several slots can share one shoot. One afternoon in a plant room and one evening training session covers about 30 slots: home-fire, svc-fire, track-fire, track-fire-wide, services-hero, the -detail close-ups, training-hero, training-cohort, training-markup, training-review, format-dubai and work-*. A second day on site covers training-site, work-site, svc-site, office-ksa and the -wide shots.
